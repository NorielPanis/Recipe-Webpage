Filipino Classic

Palabok

A beloved Filipino noodle dish smothered in a rich, savory shrimp- based sauce and topped with crispy chicharron, boiled eggs, and fresh spring onions.

Ingredients

  • 250g bihon (rice noodles)
  • 2 cups shrimp broth
  • 3 tbsp annatto oil
  • 200g ground pork
  • ⅓ cup crushed chicharron
  • 3 hard boiled eggs, sliced
  • Tinapa flakes (smoked fish)
  • Sping onions, calamansi, fish sauce

How to Cook

  1. Soak bihon noodles in warm water for 10 minutes until soft, then drain.
  2. Sauté ground pork until browned. Add shrimp broth, annatto oil, and fish sauce. Simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. Add 2 tbsp flour dissolved in water to thicken the sauce. Stir constantly until it reaches a smooth, gravy-like consistency.
  4. Blanced the softened noodles in boiling water for 1 minute. Arrange on a serving plate.
  5. Pour the sauce generously over the noodles. Top with chicharrón, egg slices, tinapa flakes, and spring onions. Served with calamansi.
